Privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-08

Hanagi is an iPhone app for keeping to-dos, schedules, birthdays, spending, documents and flights on shared boards with the people you live or travel with. This policy explains what Hanagi stores about you, why, who can see it, and what you can do about it.

Hanagi is run by Hafizh Sallam, an individual developer ("I", "me"). For any privacy question or request, email hanagiboardproject@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Hanagi stores what you put into it, so that you and the people on your boards can see it on every device.
  • There are no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and nothing is sold.
  • Anonymous usage counts and crash reports help me fix problems. They never include what you write, your amounts, names or files, and you can turn them off.
  • Receipt amounts are read on your iPhone. The receipt image only leaves your phone if you choose to save it.
  • You can delete your account from Profile at any time.

What Hanagi stores

Your account

  • Your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code or with Google.
  • If you sign in with Google, Hanagi receives your name and email address from Google. It does not receive your Google password or access to your other Google data.

Your profile

  • Your name, nickname and profile photo.
  • Your date of birth and gender, if you add them. Both are optional.

What you add to boards

  • To-dos, schedules, notes and reminder settings.
  • Birthdays, including names and dates of birth of people who do not use Hanagi.
  • Spending: amounts, currencies, categories, payment method names, notes and receipt photos.
  • Documents and other files you upload.
  • Flights: flight numbers, dates and the details looked up for them.
  • Board names, who is on each board and who is an admin.

Activity on boards

Hanagi keeps a history of changes, such as who ticked a to-do or edited a spending entry, so board members can see what happened.

Usage analytics

Hanagi counts how the app is used, for example which screens are opened, that an item was added and of what type (to-do, spending, birthday and so on), or that a Siri shortcut was used. These counts go to TelemetryDeck with your device's identifier anonymised on your phone, together with basic device details such as iPhone model, iOS version, app version and language. They never include item titles, notes, amounts, names, files or board details, and they are not linked to your account.

Crash reports

If Hanagi crashes or freezes, a crash report is sent to Sentry. It contains technical details such as where in the code the problem happened, iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a random device identifier. It does not include your name, email, screenshots or anything you added to Hanagi, and it is not linked to your account.

Turning them off

Usage counts and crash reports are on by default. To stop both, turn off Share usage and crash data in Profile. Nothing more is sent from that device until you turn it back on. The setting is kept per device.

Technical data

Like any online service, the servers that host Hanagi record basic request data, such as IP address and time, for security and to fix problems. Hanagi does not use this to build a profile of you.

On your iPhone only

  • Camera: used only when you take a photo of a receipt.
  • Receipt reading: the amount on a receipt is read by Apple's on-device text recognition. No AI service and no outside server is involved.
  • Widget, Siri and the share sheet: they use data already on your phone.
  • Offline changes are kept on your phone until you are back online, then sent.

Why it is used

Your data is used only to run Hanagi: to sign you in, to sync your items across your devices, to show board items to the board's members, to send reminders, and to keep the service secure. Usage counts and crash reports are used only to fix problems and decide what to improve. Nothing is used for advertising or sold to anyone.

Hanagi relies on your consent when you create an account and add content, and on what is necessary to provide the service you asked for.

Who can see your data

People on your boards

Items you add to a shared board are visible to its members. Board admins can manage the board, its members and its settings. A board admin can turn on an admin view that shows all to-dos on that board. Items on your personal board are visible only to you.

When you add someone's birthday, please make sure they are happy for it to be shared with the board.

Where it is stored

Hanagi's data is hosted by Supabase on cloud servers that may be outside Malaysia and Indonesia. Crash reports are stored by Sentry in the United States, and usage counts by TelemetryDeck on its own servers. By using Hanagi you agree to this transfer. Data is encrypted in transit, and access rules on the database limit each person to the boards and items they belong to.

How long it is kept

  • Your data is kept while your account exists.
  • Receipts are deleted together with their spending entry.
  • If you do not open Hanagi for 30 days, you are signed out on that device. Your data is not deleted.
  • When you delete your account, your sign-in, profile, profile photo and your files are deleted. Items you added to a shared board may stay on that board so its other members keep their records.
  • Server request logs are kept for a short period by Supabase and then removed.
  • Crash reports and usage counts are kept for a limited period set by Sentry and TelemetryDeck, then deleted.

Your rights

Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022), you can:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data Hanagi holds about you;
  • correct it (most of it you can edit directly in the app);
  • delete it, including by deleting your account in Profile;
  • withdraw consent: for usage counts and crash reports with the switch in Profile, and for everything else by deleting your account;
  • object to or ask to limit how it is used;
  • complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Email hanagiboardproject@gmail.com to make a request. I will reply within the time the law requires and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

Children

Hanagi is meant for families and households. Anyone under 18 should use it only with the permission and involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child's data was added without that permission, contact me and I will delete it.

Data breaches

If a breach affects your personal data, I will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. For important changes, I will also tell you in the app.
