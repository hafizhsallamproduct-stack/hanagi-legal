Hanagi

An iPhone app for shared boards

Hanagi keeps a household's to-dos, schedules, birthdays, spending, documents and flights on shared boards. Everyone on a board sees the same list on their own phone. You also get a personal board that only you can see.

Support

For help, feedback or privacy requests, email hanagiboardproject@gmail.com.

To delete your account, open Profile in the app and tap Delete account.
